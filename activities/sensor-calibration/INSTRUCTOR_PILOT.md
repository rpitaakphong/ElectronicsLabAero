# Private instructor preparation

This file is not included in student downloads. The existing prelab remains unchanged. Publishing is separate from building.

- Check the available UNO R4 WiFi boards, HC-SR04 modules, USB cables, rulers, breadboards, wires and flat targets. Students determine their own connections and develop or generate their own code.
- Pilot whether students can obtain raw echo durations and identify missing echoes within the suggested first 40 minutes. No supplied sketch or connection diagram is published.
- Rehearse the physical arrangement, stable target placement, chosen distance reference point and possible interference from neighboring sensors. Do not invent measurements or imply software tests establish physical performance.
- Confirm three complete runs with student-selected positions and repetition counts are feasible within the suggested 50-minute collection period. Leave two other positions out of fitting for validation.
- Test perpendicular, tilted and returned orientations while defining and controlling a target reference point. Determine whether an angle-measuring tool is available; students can instead describe and qualify an estimated tilt. There is no assigned angle or required direction of change.
- Make sure students record ruler graduation, available accuracy information, positioning bounds, alignment, timing method and shared effects before leaving. Unsupported contributions may remain explicitly unquantified. Temperature measurement is optional.
- Confirm students can create UTF-8 CSV with the illustrated headings, upload a circuit photograph and paste their Arduino code, and export their group ZIP for every member.
- Review the distinction between sensitivity, inverse conversion, regression-conditional uncertainty and a full measurement uncertainty model. Reference effects and shared effects require care; do not duplicate scatter already represented in a fit.
- The separate individual post-lab is where the instructor checks the full calculations and explanations. There is no in-class sign-off.
- Record actual physical observations here after a bench pilot. Passing software checks alone does not establish hardware readiness.
